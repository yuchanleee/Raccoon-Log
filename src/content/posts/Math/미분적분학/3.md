- 삼각함수 곱-합  

$$
\sin A\sin B
=
\frac{1}{2}
\left[
\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)
\right]
$$

$$
\cos A\cos B
=
\frac{1}{2}
\left[
\cos(A-B)+\cos(A+B)
\right]
$$

$$
\sin A\cos B
=
\frac{1}{2}
\left[
\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B)
\right]
$$

$$
\cos A\sin B
=
\frac{1}{2}
\left[
\sin(A+B)-\sin(A-B)
\right]
$$

- 역삼각함수 미분 공식 

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\sin^{-1}x\right)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}},
\qquad |x|<1
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\cos^{-1}x\right)
=
-\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}},
\qquad |x|<1
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\tan^{-1}x\right)
=
\frac{1}{1+x^2},
\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\cot^{-1}x\right)
=
-\frac{1}{1+x^2},
\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\sec^{-1}x\right)
=
\frac{1}{|x|\sqrt{x^2-1}},
\qquad |x|>1
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\csc^{-1}x\right)
=
-\frac{1}{|x|\sqrt{x^2-1}},
\qquad |x|>1
$$

- 쌍곡함수 (Hyperbolic Functions)

$$
\sinh x
=
\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}{2}
$$

$$
\cosh x
=
\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{2}
$$

- 쌍곡함수 기본 항등식 

$$
\cosh^2 x-\sinh^2 x=1
$$

$$
1-\tanh^2 x=\operatorname{sech}^2 x
$$

<br />

- 쌍곡함수 미분 공식 

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\sinh x\right)
=
\cosh x
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\cosh x\right)
=
\sinh x
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\tanh x\right)
=
\operatorname{sech}^2 x
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\coth x\right)
=
-\operatorname{csch}^2 x
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\operatorname{sech}x\right)
=
-\operatorname{sech}x\tanh x
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\operatorname{csch}x\right)
=
-\operatorname{csch}x\coth x
$$

<br />

- 역쌍곡함수 미분 공식 

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\sinh^{-1}x\right)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}},
\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\cosh^{-1}x\right)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2-1}},
\qquad x>1
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\tanh^{-1}x\right)
=
\frac{1}{1-x^2},
\qquad |x|<1
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\coth^{-1}x\right)
=
\frac{1}{1-x^2},
\qquad |x|>1
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\operatorname{sech}^{-1}x\right)
=
-\frac{1}{x\sqrt{1-x^2}},
\qquad 0<x<1
$$

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\operatorname{csch}^{-1}x\right)
=
-\frac{1}{|x|\sqrt{1+x^2}},
\qquad x\neq0
$$
